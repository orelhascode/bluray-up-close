Where the colours come from

The tracks work as a diffraction grating. A Blu-ray only shows colour when you look from a low angle with the light near your eye: its tracks are too close for any other geometry.

Reading the pits

The spot is wider than the pit on purpose. Part of the light comes back from the pit floor half a wavelength late and cancels the light from the flat land around it. In the side cut, dashed wavefronts go down and solid ones come back up.
